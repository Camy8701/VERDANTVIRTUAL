Common Questions

How does it
all work?

Practical answers to the questions most founders ask before we start working together.

Ask me directly

What does working with you actually look like day to day?

We establish a communication rhythm that works for you — typically a weekly async update and a short monthly check-in. I work independently, surface decisions that actually need you, and handle everything else without pulling you in. You get visibility without overhead.

How do you charge — hourly, retainer, or project?

Most ongoing partnerships work on a monthly retainer, with the scope and hours agreed upfront. One-off projects — such as a full SOP build or CRM setup — are scoped and priced separately. We'll establish the right structure during your discovery call once I understand what you actually need.

How quickly can you start?

Typically within 1–2 weeks of a discovery call. I take on a small number of clients at a time to protect the quality of work and attention each founder receives. If you're ready to move forward, getting in touch soon is the best first step.

Do I need to be highly involved for this to work?

The first week or two requires more of your time as I learn your business. After that, I work autonomously. You'll be involved in strategic decisions — not operational ones. The goal is for you to feel the difference in your available headspace, not add to your workload.

What industries or business types do you work with?

Service-based and consulting businesses, digital agencies, coaches and course creators, and professional service firms. The common thread is a founder who is hands-on in delivery and needs operational leverage — not just admin support.

What if things aren't working or I want to stop?

Retainers run month-to-month with 30 days notice. If something isn't working, I'd always rather know so we can address it — open, direct communication is a core part of how I work. I earn your trust every month, not just once at the start.
