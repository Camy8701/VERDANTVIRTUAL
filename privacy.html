Introduction

Verdant Virtual gathers and uses certain information about individuals in order to provide growth and operations services and to enable certain functions on this website.

We also collect information to better understand how visitors use this website and to present timely, relevant information to them.

Data We May Gather

We may collect the following information:

  • Name and business name
  • Contact information including email address and telephone number
  • Business website or URL
  • Information about the type of support you are looking for
  • Website usage data
  • Other information relevant to client enquiries

How We Use This Data

Collecting this data helps us understand what you are looking for from Verdant Virtual, enabling us to respond thoughtfully and deliver better service. Specifically, we may use data:

  • For our own internal records
  • To improve the services we provide
  • To contact you in response to a specific enquiry
  • To customise your experience on this website
  • To send you information about services, offers, or updates we think may be relevant to you
  • To contact you for market research or feedback purposes

Cookies

You can use your web browser's cookie settings to determine how our website uses cookies. If you do not want our website to store cookies on your device, set your browser to refuse cookies. Note that doing this may affect how the website functions — some pages or features may become unavailable.

Unless you change your browser settings to refuse cookies, our website will issue cookies when you visit it.

We may use cookies to:

  • Analyse web traffic using an analytics package — aggregated data helps us improve the website structure, design, content and functions
  • Store information about your preferences so the website can present you with more relevant content
  • Recognise when you return to the website so we can show relevant content or restore previously used functionality

Cookies do not provide us with access to your computer or any information about you, other than that which you choose to share with us.

Security

We will always hold your information securely. To prevent unauthorised disclosure or access to your information, we have implemented strong physical and electronic security safeguards.

We follow stringent procedures to ensure we work with all personal data in line with the Data Protection Act 1998 and applicable UK GDPR requirements.

Controlling Your Information

When you fill in a form or provide your details on our website, you may have the option to opt in to receive marketing communications from us by email.

If you have agreed that we can use your information for marketing purposes and you change your mind, simply email hello@verdantvirtual.co.uk and we will remove you promptly.

We will never lease, distribute or sell your personal information to third parties unless we have your permission or are required to by law. Any personal information we hold is stored and processed under our data protection policy, in line with the Data Protection Act 1998.

Links From Our Website

Our website may contain links to other websites. Please note that we have no control over websites outside of www.verdantvirtual.com. If you provide information to a website we link to, we are not responsible for its protection and privacy.

Be cautious when submitting data to any external website and always read the site's own data protection and privacy policies.
